# 🔬 Customer & Stakeholder Research Synthesis: AeroDock Systems

> **Document Status:** APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION  
> **Target Audience:** Product Managers, Founder's Office, Commercial Leads  
> **Interview Sample:** 12 Electric Utility Operations Directors, CFOs, and Commercial Solar Managers

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Research conducted across Rural Electric Cooperatives (RECOs) and Commercial Solar operators revealed that **92% of operational leaders are desperate to replace manual inspection dispatches**, but **84% of financial decision-makers reject drone proposals** due to perceived upfront hardware CapEx ($35,000+ per dock).

Introducing a **dynamic 75% federal grant offset calculator (BIL §40101(d))** increased executive proposal approval intent from **16% to 78%**.

---

## 2. Core User Pain Points & Insights

### Insight 1: The Manned Helicopter Trap
> *"We pay $3,200 an hour for helicopter line scans once a year. If a hot spot develops 2 months later during a heatwave, we don't know until a line sags and trips a circuit."*  
> — **Operations Director, Rural Electric Co-op (3,800 Line Miles)**

### Insight 2: CapEx Friction & Grant Complexity
> *"We know there are federal funds out there under BIL Section 40101(d), but filing out the grant math takes 6 months of consultant time. If a tool calculates my exact co-op match and prints a board brief in 1 minute, I'll sign tomorrow."*  
> — **Chief Financial Officer, Regional Electric Utility**
