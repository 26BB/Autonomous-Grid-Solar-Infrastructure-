## 1. Executive Summary & Market Positioning

AeroDock Systems accelerates critical infrastructure grid inspection by combining **Autonomous Drone-in-a-Box (DiaB) robotics** with an **interactive federal grant underwriting engine**.

The core commercial insight is that electric utility buyers (CEOs, Ops Directors, CFOs) do not hesitate due to technology skepticism—they hesitate due to **traditional CapEx procurement friction**. By structuring the purchase around **BIL §40101(d) formula grant offsets (75% match)** and **USDA Rural Utilities Service (RUS) zero-interest financing**, AeroDock converts a $35,000 CapEx outlay into a net **$8,750 net CapEx asset** with a **<3.2 month payback period**.

* **Pain:** Managing 3,500+ miles of remote distribution lines with limited lineworker crews. Manned rotor inspection contractors cost **$2,200–$3,500/hr**.
* **Value Message:** "Eliminate 90% of manned helicopter dispatches. Deploy autonomous 4K FLIR thermal patrols on a daily schedule."

### Persona 2: Chief Financial Officer (Utility / Solar Asset Operator)
* **Pain:** Reluctant to sign off on major capital equipment outlays without clear amortization schedules.
* **Value Message:** "Underwrite 75% of hardware outlays using pre-cleared BIL Section 40101(d) formula grants. Capital recovery in 2.8 months."
